About OneView

A clearer way to understand your investments.

OneView brings scattered investment records together so you can see the bigger picture without giving up control of your financial data.

Why we built it

One portfolio should feel like one portfolio.

Investment information often lives across statements, spreadsheets, and separate platforms. OneView was created to turn that fragmented history into a useful, organized view of what you own, how it is performing, and how your portfolio has changed over time.

It supports stocks, mutual funds, and VPS/pension holdings, with tools for recording activity, importing supported statements, reviewing allocation, and following returns and income.

OneView is designed and developed by Ahsan Nawaz. Visit the developer portfolio to learn more about his work.

Private by design

You stay in control

OneView is a read-only record-keeping tool. It cannot trade, move money, or access your investment accounts.

Our purpose

Make personal portfolio tracking feel calm and capable.

We are building OneView for investors who want an independent, focused record of their investments—without the noise of a trading platform.

Understand

Organize

Protect
